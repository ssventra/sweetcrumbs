About Sweet Crumb

Our story

Sweet Crumb started in 2014 with one secondhand deck oven, a sourdough starter named Gus, and a stubborn belief that every neighborhood deserves real bread. Maya baked through the night in a corner storefront on Maple Street, and by sunrise there was a line out the door for her Country Sourdough.

A decade on, we still mix every dough by hand, laminate our croissants over three days, and bake everything fresh each morning. We buy flour from a family mill two hours north and butter from a dairy down the road.

Whatever's left at closing goes to the community fridge on Elm Street. Nothing goes to waste, and nothing sits on a shelf overnight.
